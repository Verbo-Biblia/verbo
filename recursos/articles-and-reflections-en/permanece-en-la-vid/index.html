There is no victory apart from Christ

There is something we must understand clearly: there can be no victory apart from Christ.

It is not a matter of willpower. It is not a matter of isolated human discipline. It is not a matter of promising God that this time yes.

It's a matter of connection.

"Be in me, and I in you. Just as the branch cannot bear fruit of itself unless it is in the vine; so neither can you unless you are in me. I am the vine, you are the branches: he who is in me, and I in him, bears much fruit; for without me you can do nothing." John 15:4-5

Jesus said that apart from Him we can do nothing. Nothing. He did not say "you can do little." He said nothing.

And when we are disconnected from source, we are not only weak…we are empty. No substance. Sapless. No flow of life.

Many believers struggle with recurring sins—not because they love sin—but because they are spiritually malnourished. There is no constant Word. There is no intentional prayer. There is no daily communion.

And it's not about hours. It's not about religiosity. It is not about fulfilling a ritual.

It's about intentionality.

Five, ten, fifteen real minutes with God—but real—are worth more than a distracted hour. It is the heart that draws closer, not the stopwatch that runs.

The problem is not just temptation. The problem is trying to fight without staying.

A soldier without food loses strength. A believer without communion loses discernment.

When we don't fill the mind with truth, we fill it with stimulation. When we do not quiet the soul in prayer, we seek quick relief. When we do not remain on the vine, we slowly wither away.

And then we fall. We get frustrated. We feel disappointment. We move further away. And the cycle continues.

Grace is not permission to live disconnected. Grace is staying power.

You don't need to promise that you will never fail. You need to decide that you will not live apart from Him.

Victory does not begin when you leave sin. It begins when you return to the source which is Christ, and then He gives you the strength to overcome.

Remains.
