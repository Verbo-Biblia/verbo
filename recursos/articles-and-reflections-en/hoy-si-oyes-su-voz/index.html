Today, if you hear His voice

Scripture is very clear when God speaks to the heart of the believer.

"Therefore, as the Holy Spirit says: If you hear his voice today, do not harden your hearts as in the provocation, in the day of temptation in the wilderness." Hebrews 3:7-8

The apostle Paul exhorts us with direct words: "Awake, you who sleep, and arise from the dead, and Christ will give you light" (Ephesians 5:14). And the Lord Jesus Christ Himself declares: "Behold, I stand at the door and knock: if anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and will sup with him, and he with Me" (Revelation 3:20).

These texts are not addressed to people who do not know God. They are addressed to the people of God, to the church. Jesus is not calling to the world from outside; He is speaking to believers whose relationship with Him has cooled.

And that is a reality. There are times of spiritual coldness. Sometimes because of suffering, sometimes because of wear and tear, sometimes because of the natural disinterest of the flesh. We look at ourselves and don't know where to start. Then we pray and say, "Lord, lift me up. Lord, have mercy. Lord, help me."

But it is important to listen carefully to what God is really saying. Nowhere does the Lord say, "Cry out that I may raise you up." What He says is: "I stand at the door and knock." The action these texts demand is not from God; It's ours.

Hebrews says: do not harden your heart. Paul says: get up. Jesus says: open the door.

The call is clear and it is present. It's not tomorrow. It's not when you feel better. It's today. Maybe no one has told you this in a long time, but today God tells you clearly: get up.

God has already done His part. He approached. He called. Now the call is to stop, approach Him, open the door, look at Him, talk to Him, listen to Him. Let him come in, sit with you, share the table and talk.

Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your heart.
